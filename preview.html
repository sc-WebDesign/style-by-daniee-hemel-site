Meet Daniee

A stylist who locks in on exactly what you want.

Daniee runs her studio from a beautifully set-up space in Apsley, and her reviews read like thank-you notes. Clients describe her locking in on precisely the cut they had in mind, explaining what she'll do, and checking in as she works so there are no surprises at the mirror.

Colour is the signature: balayage, blended layers, fixing outgrown colour from elsewhere. More than twenty reviewers mention the comfortable, relaxing atmosphere, which is exactly what a private one-to-one studio makes possible.

  • ✦ Balayage and colour correction including growing colour out gracefully.
  • ✦ Unusual requests welcome she'll understand the brief, then nail it.
  • ✦ A proper consultation before any scissors or colour come out.

🛋️ The Studio

One chair. One client. No rush.

The studio is private and calm: no queue of dryers, no double-booking, no one waiting for your chair. Reviewers call the experience relaxing from the wash through to the final styling, and more than twenty of them mention the atmosphere specifically.

It's in Apsley, on the edge of Hemel Hempstead, with easy parking nearby. Appointments are one-to-one, so the time is entirely yours.
